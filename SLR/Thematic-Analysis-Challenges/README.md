# Thematic Analysis of Challenges


This folder contains the qualitative coding and categorization of challenges encountered in SECO-based CPS development (i.e., the deductive approach to analyze the extracted data). Analysis was performed to identify pertinent information related to our study themes, such as specific thematic units, industry-academia interactions, and emerging challenges.

## Purpose

To build an empirically grounded taxonomy of development challenges by combining insights from the literature review and survey data.

## Categories Identified

The challenges are grouped into six high-level areas:
1. System Design & Architecture
2. Quality Attributes (e.g., safety, scalability)
3. Development Processes (e.g., testing, DevOps)
4. Data and Interoperability
5. Intelligence and Automation
6. Stakeholder Engagement

## Method

Thematic analysis was conducted using spreadsheets and manual coding based on survey responses and SLR insights. Themes were consolidated through iterative discussion among authors.

## Outputs outlined in the paper

- A co-occurrence map of interrelated challenges
- Descriptions and examples for each challenge
- Support for future research and SECO design decisions

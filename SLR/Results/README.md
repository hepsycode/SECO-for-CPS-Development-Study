# Systematic Literature Review Results

This directory includes the final synthesized results from the Systematic Literature Review (SLR). This review aimed to systematically identify, analyze, and synthesize the existing body of literature to provide comprehensive insights into the current trends, challenges, and future research directions within the SECO for CPS development.

## Contents

- Aggregated data on publication types, venues, citation impact
- Domain distribution of SECO+CPS studies
- Relevance to research questions RQ1 and RQ2
- Quality scoring breakdowns by paper type

## Key Outputs

- A ranked list of 24 primary studies
- Categorized challenge areas (architecture, requirements, data, etc.)
- Co-occurrence maps between challenge types

## Methodology

Results were derived from structured data extraction and evaluation against inclusion criteria and quality benchmarks. Quantitative summaries support the construction of the final challenge taxonomy.

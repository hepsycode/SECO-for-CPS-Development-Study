# Full-Paper Reading

This subfolder contains data extracted from full-text reading of the primary studies selected through the Systematic Literature Review process. Each review offers an in-depth analysis of a selected study, focusing on its relevance, methodology, findings, and contribution to the overall research questions.

## Purpose

To thoroughly evaluate and classify each selected publication in terms of:
- Contribution type (technical, empirical, vision, review)
- Relevance to research questions
- Use case and tool presence
- Quality assessment (reporting, rigor, credibility, relevance)

## Content

- Metadata tables with quality scoring
- Notes from dual coding processes
- Review spreadsheet for 24 primary studies

## Method

Each paper was reviewed independently by at least two authors. Discrepancies were resolved through discussion. Coding focused on extracting patterns relevant to challenges in SECO for CPS development.
